# Whetū Candle Co.

- **Business name:** Whetū Candle Co.
- **Location:** Raglan, New Zealand
- **Tagline:** Scents of Aotearoa, poured by hand.

## What they do
Whetū Candle Co. hand-pours small-batch soy candles inspired by the textures, botanicals, and evening light of Aotearoa’s west coast. Each candle is designed to feel like a quiet ritual object: clean-burning soy wax, layered scent blends, tactile vessels, and a studio-made finish that feels thoughtful rather than mass-produced. The brand sits between home fragrance and slow living homeware — candles made to be lit, gifted, styled, and remembered.

## Target audience
Design-conscious millennials in New Zealand and Australia who care about how their homes feel as much as how they look. They buy from local makers, prefer sustainable materials over fast homeware, and are drawn to earthy, understated objects with a story. They are often renters and first-home owners, interior styling enthusiasts, boutique gift shoppers, and hospitality buyers looking for elevated wholesale scent products with a grounded regional identity.

## Brand voice
Warm, grounded, artisanal, and quietly confident. The brand speaks with softness and clarity — never overly precious, never corporate. It feels tactile, local, and honest, with a slightly poetic coastal sensibility.

## Pages needed
- **Home:** A visually rich welcome page with candle lifestyle imagery, a concise brand introduction, featured scents, and a strong invitation to explore or enquire.
- **About:** The maker story, studio process, ingredients, and sustainability commitments presented with warmth and credibility.
- **Shop / Contact:** A six-product showcase grid paired with wholesale enquiry details and a contact form for stockists, stylists, and collaborators.

- Home page must include featured scents and an about snippet.
